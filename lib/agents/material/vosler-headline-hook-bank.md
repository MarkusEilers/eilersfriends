## 2 · Kern-Templates (VS-1 bis VS-12)

- **VS-1** "The [noun] Who Perfected The [topic]" → expandiert: "Meet The [Real Estate Agent] Who Perfected The [Short Sale], and Learn How it 4x'd His Agency's Profits in 6 Months."
- **VS-2 (Fear of Loss):** "The (Astonishingly Simple) Reason [Audience] Often Ignore [Topic], and How Many Miss Out On [Zahl]% Higher Profits."
- **VS-3 (Fear of Change):** "New Scientific Approach is Teaching [Audience] How to Unlock The Secret Power of [Topic]… Without [Cannibalizing Their Core Offer]."
- **VS-4 (Sub-Title):** "A [adjective] Approach to [enticing promise]" — Archetypen: Powerful Promise / Contrarian / Intriguing / Inspiring.
- **VS-5 (Even-if-Opener):** "Even if you've found that XYZ hasn't worked for you in the past, let me show you how a simple change in perspective can make all the difference."
- **VS-6 (Boogie-Man):** "The [enemy] [problem with] [common belief] of x._____ is not y._____, it's z._____." — "Your product isn't 'not selling' because of its quality… It's not selling because your messaging isn't connecting with your audience."
- **VS-7 (Challenger):** "You don't need a [thing they think they need], you need a [reframed real solution]!"
- **VS-8 (Contrarian-Opener):** "Most people assume [common belief]. The truth is… [contrarian claim]. Read on to learn how…"
- **VS-9 (Imagination-Trigger):** "Imagine what [benefit] could mean for you and your business."
- **VS-10 (Benefit-of-Benefit):** "Tired of [embarrassing consequence] because of [surface problem]!? It's time you got [emotional payoff]…"
- **VS-11 (Trap-Naming-Cover):** "Get Out of the '[Painful Named Trap]'… Learn How To [Attract Better X that Pay What You're Worth]! & Maybe Even Unlock [Secondary Dream Benefit]." / Promise-Variante: "Finally, put an end to the '[named trap]' by [big benefit] while [removing pain]."
- **VS-12 (Nostalgie-Sequenz):** "A lot has changed, but, like you, we still believe… that ____ is still true. It may seem old fashioned to some, but I think you'd agree… Remember when…? Wouldn't it be great if…? Well that's why we…"

## 6 · Subject-Lines & Opener (S. 289-290)

- **Report:** "New {institute} approved {process} + {benefit}" / "Introducing {technique} + {benefit or mystery}"
- **Data:** "{Percentage} + {unexpected thing}" / "{Trendy thing} {percentage change}"
- **How-To:** "{Attention-grabber}: how to {avoid/get it}" / "How {world-class example or average joe} {does amazing thing}"
- **Empty Suitcase:** "{Name}, this is for you" / "I learned this from watching ___" / "I {past-tense verb} this. The world changed."
- **Announcement:** "Introducing {Name}: {short value prop}" / "New! {Benefit without name}"
- **Scarcity+Urgency:** "Only {#} {days} left to {X}" / "Last chance to {action}" / "Get {valuable thing} if you {action} in the next {#} {days}"
- Bewährte Beispiel-Subjects aus dem Buch: "can going to college ruin your life?" · "secret behind 20m in revenue by 27 + (one important shift in perspective)" · "Welcome, you're gonna love this…" · "the small business problem" · "Sleeping on the tennis court" · "Sarah's Story" · "Let's Talk Solution"

## 7 · CTA-, Offer- & Testimonial-Formeln (S. 288, 292)

- **I Want Button:** "I want to ___" / "Get ___" / "Show me ___"
- **Hook, Line and Sinker:** Command verb + offer + urgency.
- **Elements of the Offer:** (0. "Here's What You're Gonna Get") 1. Establish the Value → 2. Conditional Bonus → 3. Price → 4. Trivialize Price → 5. Guarantee → 6. Risk Reversal → 7. Scarcity.
- **Price-Anchor-Kaskade:** "While many charge $5k or more… So I don't charge $10k, or $5k, or even $2k…"
- **Testimonials:** Before-After-Experience · TEASE-Check (Tactful, Emphasizes strength, Authentic, Short, Engages) · Die 4 S: Specific, Short, Sizzling, Signed.
- **Facebook-Ad-CTAs:** "See why {influencer} said {intriguing thing}" · "Free {high-value freebie}" · "Need {desirable thing}? {Actionable solution}" · "Sick of {thing they're sick of}?" · "Stop {unpleasant thing}"

## 8 · Struktur-Formeln (Kompendium, S. 277-292)

- **AIDA / AIDCA / IDCA** (Conviction via Testimonials, Endorsements, Statistik, Demo, Garantie)
- **PAS** (Kennedy: "the most reliable sales formula ever invented") + Variante **PADS** (…Discredit other solutions…)
- **4 Ps:** Picture – Promise – Prove – Push (alt: Problem – Promise – Proof – Proposal)
- **QUEST:** Qualify → Understand → Educate → Stimulate → Transition
- **ACCA · AAPPA/PAPA · SLAP · AIDPPC (Collier) · AICPBSAWN** (Attention, Interest, Credibility, Proof, Benefits, Scarcity, Action, Warn, Now)
- **Jack Lacey 5-Point:** What will you do for me? How? Who's responsible? Who have you done this for? What will it cost?
- **Star-Story-Solution** · **7-Step** (Key Benefit → build → specifics → proof → risks of not acting → summary → CTA) · **9-Point** · **Perry Belcher 21-Part** (Call out → Big Promise → Backup → Painful Problem → Solution → Cost of development → Ease → Speed → Future Pace → Credentials …)
- **HELLYEAH:** Holler → Empathize → Lambast → Legwork → Yes-Solution → Educate → Action → Handle doubts
- **P.A.S.T.O.R (Ray Edwards):** Person/Problem/Pain → Amplify → Story/Solution/System → Testimonials → Offer (80% Transformation!) → Response
- **12-Step Foolproof Sales Letter (Frey):** Attention → Problem → Solution → Credentials → Benefits → Social Proof → Offer → Scarcity → Guarantee → CTA → Warning → Reminder-Close (überwindet die 7 Einwand-Typen, siehe Methods §13)
- **VSL (Jim Edwards):** Shocking Statement → Problem+Warum wichtig → Agitate → tiefes emotionales Level → Solution → Credibility → Proof → Biggest Things They Get → Act-Now-Gründe → Close. **Common VSL Template:** Greeting → Problem+Promise → Scarcity → Agitate → THE Solution → Features+Benefits → CTA 1 (Desire) → Credentials → Guarantee → CTA 2 (Logik) → FOMO-Warnung → CTA 3 (FOMO).
- **Value Proposition:** "For {target} who {need}, {Name} is {category} that {benefit}." · VAD-Varianten ("We help X do Y doing Z", "{Proven industry example} for {new domain}")
- **Email-Sequenzen:** Walling 5-Day-Drip · Wishpond 5-Part · **PASOP** (jede Mail endet mit neuem Problem als Cliffhanger) · 6-Email-New-Customer-Nurturing (Welcome+Support → Free Offer → Case Study → ROI → Testimonial-Videos → FAQs)
- **Blog:** Michael Hyatt 6-Part · **HIPASI** (Headline → Image → Problem → Agitation → Solution → Invitation)
- **Optimierung: SCAMPER** (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to other use, Eliminate, Rearrange) · **A FOREST**-Check (Alliteration, Facts, Opinions, Repetition, Examples, Statistics, Three)
- **Gary Halbert Newsletter:** Attention → Why interested → Why believe → Prove → Itemize benefits → How to order → Order now
- **7 Deadly Sins als Emotions-Raster:** Lust→Desire · Gluttony→Self-interest · Greed→Possessiveness · Sloth→Laziness ("do less work") · Wrath→Anger · Envy→Jealousy ("rise above your Joneses") · Pride→Confidence
