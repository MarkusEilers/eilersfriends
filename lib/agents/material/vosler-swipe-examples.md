# Sean Vosler · Swipe-Beispiele (verbatim)

Quelle: "7 Figure Marketing Copy" V5 (Nutzungsrechte erworben). Komplette Copy-Beispiele mit Anmerkung, was sie demonstrieren. Nutzung: **Struktur, Rhythmus und Mechanik imitieren — nie wörtlich in Kunden-Copy übernehmen** (Voslers eigene Regel, S. 54) und immer durch Voice-Charter + Forbidden-Words filtern.

## 1 · "Even if"-Intro (S. 38) — Jugular Objection im ersten Satz entwaffnen

> Headline: "The exercise program that helps even the most time crunched lose weight!"
>
> "Even if you don't have hours to spend at the gym, there's a new program that can work for you. I used to get overwhelmed thinking about the time commitment I'd have to make if I want to reach my fitness goals, I'm sure you can relate. Time is something we all could use more of, and with all the demands of daily life it can be easy to conclude that if something's going to get cut, it's exercise. But here's the truth, you can accomplish so much more, in so much less time, if you simply take a few moments to learn the '80/20 rule of fitness.' Below you'll find the routine even the most time crunched person can use to get noticeable results, fast!"

Demonstriert: Even-if-Opener → Empathie → Reframe → Tease des Inhalts.

## 2 · Barnum-Statement + Quiz-Segmentierung (S. 71-72) — Charity

> "...you're the kind of person who understands the importance of community, imagine if everything you knew and loved was in the path of a raging inferno? No doubt you would be scared, angry, and want to do everything you could to save what you love."

> Quiz: "How 'Community Conscious' Are You? Your score may surprise you..." (3 Fragen, Skala 1-10) → an die High-Interest-Gruppe:
> "Congratulations! You're clearly the type of person who cares about their community, the world needs more people like you. […] Today only a very generous donor has agreed to a dollar-for-dollar match of any amount contributed […] Your contribution is a powerful way to say 'YES! I care!'…"

Demonstriert: validierendes "you", Selbst-Segmentierung, CTA als Identitäts-Bestätigung.

## 3 · Staged FAQ — validate → answer → reframe (S. 78)

> Frage: "Doesn't this take a lot of time to implement before I see results?"
> Antwort: "This is a very common concern, after all, you're dealing with these problems now and need help! […] that's why we've created a 'Day 1: Fast Action' quick start section of the training to help you start seeing results right away! […] While it can be tempting to search for a fast (but temporary) solution that will inevitably fail, it's clear that the best course of action is to invest in a program designed to last for the long haul."

## 4 · Anchoring-Close (S. 85)

> "Clearly you can see how valuable this system is for your own business! How much would it cost you to hire a full time employee to do this for you? $50,000, $75,000, $100,000? Even then you could justify the cost couldn't you? Well we've made it much easier, and much much more affordable for you at the low cost of only $2000!"

Anker 1: Mitarbeiter-Preis. Anker 2: "selbst dann gerechtfertigt".

## 5 · "Attack Their Enemies" komplett in einem Absatz — Bed Bug Bomb (S. 145)

> Do you have nasty bed bugs? {struggle enemy}
> We hate {value} those seemingly impossible to get rid of monsters as much as you do!
> The problem with most sprays on the market today isn't just that they aren't as effective as they used to be {boogie-man, lokal}, it's that they can be astonishingly unhealthy {boogie-man, universal}!
> That's why we created Bed Bug Bomb. The fastest way to get rid of bed bugs when nothing else seems to work, period! {solution}

Demonstriert: Feind definieren → Werte-Allianz → lokale→universale Tension → Offer.

## 6 · Contrarian-E-Mail komplett — "can going to college ruin your life?" (S. 164)

> Subject: can going to college ruin your life?
>
> Couldn't help but stop and glance through this recent Consumers Report magazine with that quote on the cover... I've talked about it before in some of my videos, but it seems the opinion that college might not be for everyone is becoming more and more of a common opinion.
>
> It really makes you wonder... Is there really any alternative to going into massive debt just for the HOPE that I might have a better future?
>
> I like to think, and have seen it happen many many times, that there are great alternatives IF you're willing to work hard, and learn hard. […]
>
> Maybe you went to college and can relate to the woman quoted on the cover with $152,000 in college debt. […]
>
> It also should make us consider alternative methods of learning valuable skills as a place to START on our quest for success instead of looking to traditional education systems. Not simply because of the astronomical cost and low return of most degrees, but also because the potential for success is much higher on average for those who pave their own way.
>
> Mentorship is an old school approach to learning […] the return on the time and effort can be much higher.

Mapping: Subject = Attention · Abs. 1 = Acknowledgement · Alternative-Frage = Aggravate + Open Loop · Magazin-Story = Authority · Kosten/Schulden = Angst · Mentorship = Ambiguity(-Auflösung).

## 7 · KLTCC-Kampagne — die 5 Original-Mails (S. 213-217, gekürzt auf die tragenden Passagen)

**#1 Know — "Welcome, you're gonna love this…":** Dank + Programm-Vorschau → "BEFORE we dive into the tools… it'd be a good idea for us to get to know each other a bit better! My name's Sean Vosler, and I'm a lot like you…" (Familie, Corgi — Nahbarkeit) → Kern-Lehre "it's ALL about focus" → CTA verkauft NUR das Video ("Grab a pen and paper and set aside 5 minutes…") → PS: Engagement-Frage auf der FB-Wall → PPS: Cliffhanger auf Mail 2 ("'the small business problem'… keep an eye out").

**#2 Like — "the small business problem":** Rückbezug auf Mail 1 → universale Wahrheit "A business isn't a business without SALES" → eigene Verlorenheit ("I felt frozen… but once I shifted my focus to SALES, all the pieces started to come together") → Werte ("I'm a big believer in once you understand a problem it's your responsibility to fix it") → expliziter Cliffhanger: "Hate to leave you with a cliffhanger but the solution is a WHOLE email in itself…" → Trost-Content (kurzes Video).

**#3 Trust — "Sleeping on the tennis court":** Tiefpunkt-Story ("It doesn't seem that long ago that I found myself literally sleeping on a tennis court…") → heutige Position → Reframe "money is a TOOL, nothing else" → Engagement-CTA (Meinung teilen) → Cliffhanger: "Tomorrow I'm going to share Sarah's story… Chances are, you're probably in the same boat."

**#4 Challenge — "Sarah's Story":** Avatar-Story (Webdesignerin tauscht 9-to-5 gegen 5-to-9, wird Buchhalterin+Support+Marketing in einem, Sales sinken) → "Chances are you've experienced this to some measure" → Challenge: "take a look at your business from 40,000 feet… what would YOU change right now?" + Diagnose-Liste (Traffic? Funnels? Quellen? Fokus?) → Glaubens-Angriff: "The traditional mindset is to simply POWER THROUGH the problems. What you really need to do is systematize your business. This is what 100% of successful businesses do." → Cliffhanger auf die Auflösung.

**#5 Convert — "Let's Talk Solution":** Rückgriff auf Sarah → Ehrlichkeit: "there's no such thing as a SIMPLE solution in this case, but there is a smarter solution." → Anaphora: "The smarter solution is to learn from others who have already solved the problem. / The smarter solution is to use tools to reach your customers that they want you to use! / The smarter solution is to learn from the experts." → Experten-Social-Proof gestapelt (Howes/Porterfield/Wedmore mit konkreten Zahlen) → Offer → Risk Reversal: "'water bottle investment' of $1 – we're taking all the risk here" → Scarcity ("For support reasons, we can take on only so many members at the $1 trial price") → Bonus (73% Lifetime) → "#1 Reason You Should Sign Up Today: Most people aren't willing to… INVEST IN THEMSELVES, this is your chance to take that first step!" → Garantie.

## 8 · "Feast"-Sales-Letter — die Architektur-Passagen (S. 228-250)

**Cover/Promise:** "Get Out of the 'Freelance Feast or Famine' Trap… Learn How To Attract Better Clients that Pay You What You're Worth! & Maybe Even Unlock That 'Free Time' We Were All Promised."

**The Abyss (Opener, S. 230)** — Schmerz-Szene in Ich-Form:
> Another Monday, my eyes creak open... 5:00 am… The buzzer laughs at my joke of a sleep schedule. Or was it Wednesday? This isn't what I signed up for. I quit my job to unplug that dang clock, not turn it back, earlier & earlier.
> My phone buzzed. I hate that little red number on the envelope. Why does it have to be so red? […] Coffee first, out of creamer again. Great.
> […] McKinsey. He needs to understand he hired me to do a job. That slob seems to think he hired me to tell me how to do my job. […]
> I press the button. An optimistic hum rose from my Mac, chipping that familiar jingle. That jingle used to signal a day of creation was ahead. I used to bask in that tone, I used to enjoy it. Now it only signaled jumping back on the wheel. It is Wednesday, and no amount of coffee is going to help…
> It's 5:42am… time to get back on the wheel.

**Prelude (S. 231):** Trap benennen ("This is the 'feast or famine' cycle… and it's time to escape, once and for all") → Promise ("It's time I shared the insights that helped me go from six figure years to six figure months") → Qualifikation ("You'll know your current strategy isn't working if…").

**The Revelation (S. 236)** — gespiegelter Payoff zum Abyss (dasselbe Mac-Knopf-Bild, invertiert):
> I could hardly believe the flickering numbers on my screen. There was the biggest deposit I've ever seen in my account […] I had generated close to 10 times that amount in PROFIT for my client. […]
> I pressed the button. The fans in my Mac gently whirled to a halt. A wonderful silence remained.
> I couldn't help thinking back to the McKinsey days, maybe I judged him too harshly. It wasn't his fault that he felt like he needed to micromanage our project. It was my fault. I didn't convince him I knew what I was doing. […] It wasn't about what I was creating…. It was about what my creation did for them.

**Atonement (S. 246):** "I wish someone would have shared this strategy with me sooner." → Credibility ("north of $100m in sales") → Origin ("I used to sell 'websites'… always chasing the next sale… burned out and overwhelmed").

**Offer-Transition + Preis-Anker (S. 249):** "We've explored the 'what' and 'why', but what about the 'how'? […] While many charge $5k or more for hands on coaching & training of this kind… So I don't charge $10k, or $5k, or even $2k for this program. Before we get to the pricing let me share with you what you get when you join today…"

Architektur-Lehre: Abyss (Schmerz-Szene) → Prelude (Promise) → Teile 1-4 (Teach/Challenge, je mit Alt-Weg/Neu-Weg-Recap) → Revelation (Wendepunkt, gespiegelte Bilder) → Atonement (Autor-Story) → Offer → Anker → CTA.

## 9 · Story-Miniaturen

**Riptide (S. 171)** — Erfahrung übertragen statt Fakten nennen:
> "At first, it seemed like any other wave, except that no matter how hard I swam I couldn't get any closer to the shore. I was being dragged away from safety, channeled toward the deep... dark... blue of the abyss. […] But then, it hit me! I remembered what my parents had taught me - swim with the current and out and around its grasp. With that memory, I felt invigorated with hope as I navigated around the current and back to the safety of the shore."

**Doctor/Coffee-Close (S. 190)** — Autorität + Angst-Anerkennung + Social Proof + Open Loop:
> "I can see you're worried about quitting drinking coffee, I know it'll be a challenge but here's 30 other people who went through the same thing and reached their goal successfully; and you know what? They actually enjoy not being addicted to coffee […] Let me show you how they did it..."

**Don Draper "Carousel" (S. 91/162)** — Contrarian-Struktur mündlich; lokales Problem ("das Rad einbauen") auf universale Ebene heben ("It's not called the wheel. It's called the carousel… a place where we know we're loved").

**Dove (S. 184)** — Commodity auf universalen Wert heben: "We believe beauty should be a source of confidence, and not anxiety. That's why we are here to help women everywhere develop a positive relationship with the way they look…"
